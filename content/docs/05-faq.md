# Frequently asked questions

## What does 7C Labs do?
7C Labs gives you one platform for the whole software lifecycle, from architecture and cost planning to secure delivery and observability, with AI agents doing the routine work and people approving what matters. We also offer services to assess, implement, operate and train.

## Which cloud platforms do you support?
We support AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud, with one operating model for identity, network and cost controls across all of them.

## How much does it cost? What is the pricing?
Pricing and cost depend on the scope of the engagement and the products you use. Contact contact@7clabs.com for a quote.

## How do AI agents and human approvals work?
An AI agent works each stage of the lifecycle, and a named person approves every step that matters, such as signing off the architecture, the cost estimate, an infrastructure plan or a production release. You choose how much each agent may do on its own: suggest only, act with approval, or act within guardrails.

## How do you monitor AI usage?
AI Monitor shows which models each team and service uses, what it costs, how fast it responds and whether sensitive data appears in prompts or outputs, with alerts for spikes and quality changes.
