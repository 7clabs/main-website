# About 7C Labs and careers

## Who is behind 7C Labs?
7C Labs is built by a team of experienced engineers who have spent their careers in platform engineering, deploying solutions to meet business objectives. We built 7C Labs to make that effort lighter: AI agents do the repeatable work, secure and compliant by default, and people keep every decision.

## What is your vision?
Every team ships fast and safe, with nothing left to chance.

## Are you hiring? Do you offer internships?
Yes. We have intern positions for people with development or platform understanding who want to help simplify how full-fledged, secure and compliant platforms are managed. Open roles are Platform Engineering Intern, Full-Stack Development Intern, and DevSecOps and Cloud Security Intern. See the Careers page on the website, or email contact@7clabs.com to apply.

## What do interns work on?
Interns work alongside our engineers on the platform itself, for example infrastructure-as-code and pipelines, product screens and APIs for agents and approvals, or security and compliance automation and AI usage monitoring. Duration and other details are shared when we speak.
