# About 7C Labs

## Who we are
7C Labs is the trading name of 7CLABS ENTERPRISE PRIVATE LIMITED. We build a platform and offer services that cover the software lifecycle from development to deployment, with DevSecOps and SRE principles built in and clear monitoring of how AI is used across cloud platforms.

## What problem we solve
Teams often stitch together separate tools for security scanning, delivery pipelines, reliability, cloud cost and AI usage. That leaves gaps between stages, slow approvals, and no single view of risk or cost. 7C Labs connects these stages so every change can be traced from design to production, with security checks and human approvals at the points that matter.

## Who it is for
Engineering leaders, platform and DevOps teams, security teams, SRE and operations teams, and finance or FinOps owners who need to see what their cloud and AI usage costs.
